EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## The short version

- This website sets **no cookies of its own** — none at all, essential or otherwise.
- There is **no analytics, no tracking pixel and no advertising script** anywhere on the site.
- The [savings calculator](/en/savings-calculator/) works **without giving us anything**. It runs in
  your browser and no result is ever sent to us.
- Nothing on this site asks you to create an account.

## What is collected

### Hosting logs

paton.ch is a static website hosted on Firebase Hosting, a service of Google. Like any web server,
it records technical request data — the IP address of the request, the time, the page requested,
the referring page and the browser's user agent — for the purposes of delivering the site,
diagnosing faults and defending against abuse.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a secure and functioning website
(Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not combine this data with anything else and we do not use it to build
profiles. Google acts as our processor for hosting; Google may transfer data outside Switzerland and
the EEA under its own safeguards. Retention follows Google's Firebase Hosting log retention, which
is short-term and measured in days, and we do not export or archive these logs.

### Email you send us

If you write to [info@paton.ch](mailto:info@paton.ch), we process what you send in order to reply —
your address, your name if you give it, and the content of your message
(Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We keep business correspondence for as long as the exchange is live and
thereafter for as long as Swiss commercial record-keeping obligations require, then delete it.

### The savings calculator

The calculator collects nothing. Every figure is computed in your browser and never leaves it.

The page offers one optional action: if you type an email address and choose *Email me this
summary*, your browser opens **your own email application** with the figures already written into a
draft addressed to you. Nothing is submitted to a server, the address is not sent to us, and we
never see it unless you choose to email us separately. If you do not use that button, no address is
entered anywhere.

## What is not collected

We do not use Google Analytics or any other analytics service, we do not embed social media widgets
or share buttons that load third-party code, we do not run advertising or remarketing tags, and we
do not operate a newsletter from this site. Earlier versions of paton.ch did use analytics and
embedded social widgets; the site was rebuilt from scratch and none of that was carried over.

## Fonts

The site loads the Manrope typeface from Google Fonts. Your browser makes a request to Google's
font servers to fetch it, and that request exposes your IP address to Google. If you would rather
that did not happen, a browser extension that blocks third-party requests, or a content blocker,
will prevent it — the site remains fully usable, in a fallback system typeface.

## Your rights

You may ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, ask us
to restrict how we process it, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and — where GDPR
applies — receive it in a portable form. Write to [info@paton.ch](mailto:info@paton.ch).

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority: in Switzerland, the Federal Data
Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the EU, your national authority.

## Security

The site is served over HTTPS only. Because we hold almost nothing, there is almost nothing to
lose — which is the point. Note that email is not an encrypted channel; please do not send patient
data or anything confidential to us by ordinary email.

## Changes

If we ever add something that collects data — analytics, a contact form, a newsletter — this policy
will be updated first, and anything non-essential will be placed behind an explicit consent choice
before it runs.
